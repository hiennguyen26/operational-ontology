# Volunteer handbook (excerpt)

## Stewards
Bed stewards (some members still say bed captains) water and weed one bed each week.
Each steward tends the tomatoes and the mint in the north bed from May to September.
The plot coordinator reads the harvest log every Saturday before the shares are split.

## Garden rules
No pesticides are used anywhere in the garden, on any bed.
Mulch keeps the soil moist between waterings.

## Contact
Questions go to the coordinator desk at garden-desk@allotment.invalid or (555) 555-0142.
